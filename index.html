Research Interests

I develop signal processing, machine learning, and AI methods to recover physiologically meaningful signals and derive digital physiomarkers from complex biosignals. My research focuses on the objective assessment of pain and autonomic states using electrodermal activity and ECG-derived skin nerve activity.
